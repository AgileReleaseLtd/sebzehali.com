# s-017: Provinces that refuse connections from outside Turkey

- Status: open
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-10-30

## What

Fetch the hal bulletins of İstanbul, Kocaeli, Eskişehir, Aydın, Muğla, Malatya and Van from a machine with a
Turkish IP address (a small Turkish VPS or a self-hosted GitHub runner in Turkey), then add them as sources.

## Why

These seven publish prices, but their servers time out from the UK and from GitHub's runners (checked
2026-10-30 with a one-off workflow run from GitHub's servers; results below). They include the country's largest
markets: İstanbul's three halls (Bayrampaşa, Ataşehir, Gürpınar fish), Aydın's per-district bulletins
(Acarlar, Nazilli) and Muğla's combined bulletin for Fethiye and Ortaca.

## Done when

The seven are fetched daily and appear on the site, or each one is ruled out with a reason.

## Notes

- Known pages: İstanbul `halfiyatlaripublicdata.ibb.gov.tr` and the CKAN dataset
  `data.ibb.gov.tr/dataset/hal-urunleri-ve-fiyatlari-web-servisi`; Kocaeli `kocaeli.bel.tr/hal-fiyatlari/`;
  Eskişehir `eskisehir.bel.tr/hal-fiyatlari`; Aydın `aydin.bel.tr` (PDFs under `/Content/files/hal_fiyat/`);
  Muğla `mugla.bel.tr/halfiyatlari`; Malatya `malatya.bel.tr/hal-fiyatlari/`; Van `van.bel.tr/Syf/hal-fiyatlari.html`.
- Never reuse login values embedded in a page (İstanbul's website embeds some).
- Cost and upkeep of the Turkish machine are the question: a VPS, or a runner that pushes data to the repo.
- One-off GitHub Actions check, 2026-10-30 10:39 UTC (the workflow's branch was deleted afterwards): every one of
  İstanbul (site and CKAN), Kocaeli, Aydın, Muğla, Malatya and Van timed out after 40 s; Eskişehir timed out in the
  TLS handshake. In the same run all the sources now on the site answered, as did Konya Ereğli and hal.gov.tr.
